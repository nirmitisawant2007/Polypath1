For first-year diploma students in Maharashtra, the MSBTE Scholarship (specifically the "MSBTE Scholarship for Meritorious Students") is a prestigious financial aid program aimed at supporting students who have excelled in their 10th-grade (SSC) exams.  
Here is the essential information you need for the 2025–26 academic year:
1. Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for the MSBTE scholarship in your first year, you must meet these conditions:
Academic: You must have secured at least 70% marks (or equivalent grade) in your SSC (10th) examination.  
Admission: You must be a regular student admitted to an AICTE-approved diploma course in a government, aided, or unaided polytechnic affiliated with MSBTE.
Income: Your annual family income should not exceed ₹8 Lakh.  
Exclusivity: You should not be receiving any other government scholarship (like MahaDBT, EBC, or Minority scholarships) at the same time.
2. Scholarship Amount
The scholarship typically provides ₹7,000 per year. This amount is intended to cover tuition fees and other educational expenses.
3. Application Process
The application is usually handled through the official MSBTE portal rather than the standard MahaDBT site.
Online Registration: Visit the official MSBTE website.
Form Submission: Fill out the scholarship application form during the window (usually announced in the first semester).
Institute Verification: Submit a hard copy of the application and required documents to your college's scholarship desk for verification.
