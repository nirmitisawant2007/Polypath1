The MSBTE Academic Calendar 2025–26 provides the roadmap for your entire academic year. Since the Winter 2025 results were just released on January 2nd, you are now entering the Even Semester (2nd, 4th, and 6th semesters) phase.
Below is the schedule for the Summer 2026 session:
1. Academic Term Dates (Even Semester)
Commencement of Term: December 15, 2025
First Class Test (Unit Test I): January 27 – January 29, 2026
Second Class Test (Unit Test II): March 30 – April 2, 2026
Conclusion of Term: April 4, 2026
2. Examination Schedule (Summer 2026)
This is the period when you will be appearing for your final semester-end assessments.
3.Exam Form Filling Deadlines
As mentioned earlier, these dates are strictly enforced. Missing them will result in heavy penalties or being barred from the exam.
Normal Fee: February 2 – February 12, 2026

4. Key Holidays and Breaks
While the board sets the academic schedule, individual colleges may vary slightly on minor holidays. However, the Summer Vacation is typically scheduled after the theory exams in late May until the start of the next academic year on July 1, 2026.
Tip for 1st Year Students: The transition from 1st to 2nd semester is fast. Your First Class Test is coming up at the end of this month (January 27th). Ensure your journals and practical manuals are up to date, as you won't get much time between the conclusion of the term and the practical exams in April.
