The K-Scheme is the latest curriculum introduced by MSBTE (aligned with NEP 2020). It follows an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) pattern, which means marks are divided between continuous assessment and final exams to ensure you gain practical skills, not just theoretical knowledge.
Here is the breakdown of the marking scheme for a typical 1st-year subject:
1. Theory Assessment (Total: 100 Marks)
Theory marks are usually split into two parts:
Summative Assessment (SA-TH): 70 Marks
This is your final "End Semester Exam" conducted by the board.
Passing Marks: Typically 28 out of 70 (40%).
Formative Assessment (FA-TH): 30 Marks
This is the average of two Unit Tests (Class Tests) conducted by your college.
Each test is for 30 marks, and the final 30 marks are the average of the two.
2. Practical Assessment
Practical subjects are evaluated differently to ensure you are actually performing the experiments.
Summative Assessment (SA-PR): 25 to 50 Marks
This is the final external/internal practical exam.
Formative Assessment (FA-PR): 25 to 50 Marks
This is your Term Work. It is based on your continuous performance in the lab, manual completion, and attendance.
Warning: If you do not secure the minimum passing marks in FA-PR, you will be "Detained" for that semester.
3. Self-Learning Assessment (SLA)
A unique feature of the K-Scheme is the SLA.
These are marks (usually 25) allocated for micro-projects, assignments, or presentations.
If you fail the SLA, you fail the entire course and must resubmit the work.
