Filling out the MSBTE exam form is a critical process that usually happens twice a year: once for the Winter (Odd Semester) exams and once for the Summer (Even Semester) exams.
Since it is currently January 2026, the Winter 2025 results were just declared (January 2nd), and the window for the Summer 2026 exam forms is opening soon.
1. Important Dates (Summer 2026)
Based on the official academic calendar, here are the expected dates for filling your Summer 2026 exam forms:
Normal Fee Period: February 2 – February 12, 2026
With Late Fee (₹200): February 14 – February 15, 2026
With Penalty (₹1500): February 17 – February 19, 2026
2. Step-by-Step Filling Process
Login: Go to the MSBTE Official Portal and click on the "Exam Form" link. You will need your Enrollment Number and a Password (usually provided by your college's exam cell).
Verify Details: Once logged in, verify your personal details (Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name) and your course information.
Select Subjects: Ensure all the subjects you are appearing for (both Theory and Practical/Oral) are correctly listed.
Submit & Print: Click "Submit" to generate the exam form. Download and print the form immediately.
College Confirmation: Take the printed copy and your exam fees to your college's office/exam cell. Your form is not valid until the college confirms it on their portal.
3. Exam Fees
Regular Fee: Typically around ₹600 for standard diploma courses.
Late Fee: An additional ₹200 if you miss the first deadline.
Super Penalty: If you miss even the late fee deadline, the penalty can go up to ₹1500.
4. Checklist of Requirements
Enrollment Number: This is your permanent ID for the entire diploma.
Passport Photo/Signature: Usually, these are already in the system from your enrollment, but verify they are visible.
Fees: Ensure you have the exact amount in the format required by your college (online payment, cash, or challan).
Crucial Tip: Always check your subjects carefully before clicking submit. If there is a mistake in the elective subjects or backlog papers, only your college can rectify it through the RBTE (Regional Board of Technical Education).
